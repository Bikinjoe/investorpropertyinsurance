This is the InvestorPropertyInsurance blog, run by Vantage Point Risk Partners. We publish content for real estate investors across the western US: landlords, STR hosts, multifamily owners, commercial property investors, and active rehabbers.

## What we cover

We write about:

- DP-3 coverage and how it differs from homeowner policies
- Short-term rental coverage (Airbnb, VRBO) and platform protection gaps
- Multifamily and commercial coverage structure
- Builders risk and the construction-to-permanent transition
- Loss of rents, ordinance and law, and the high-value optional coverages
- Vacancy clauses, rehab coverage, and how to insure properties that aren't currently rented
- Carrier selection: REInsurePro, Steadily, Obie, plus the standard markets

What we don't write: generic real estate advice, content that has nothing to do with insurance, AI-generated fluff. Every post answers a question we actually get from investors.

## Who writes this

Posts come from us, Vantage Point Risk Partners. We're an independent insurance agency in Eugene, Oregon. We work with 10+ carrier markets and we focus on real estate investor business. Serving AZ, CA, CO, ID, MT, NM, NV, OR, TX, UT, WA, and WY.
